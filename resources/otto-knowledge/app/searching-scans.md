# Searching scans with Otto

Examples:

- `Show me the most recent scans from today`
- `Find scans for ABC1234`
- `Show plate 33EZE* in the last 7 days`
- `Scans for XYZ991 today`

You do not need a plate, driver, or kit — a time range (or “recent”) is enough. Otto defaults to the last 30 days when you omit a range. Results appear in an interactive table under Otto’s reply.
