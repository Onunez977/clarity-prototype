# Searching hits with Otto

When you say **latest** and do not name a time range, Otto searches hits from the **last 12 hours**. An explicit range still wins — `latest hits today` uses today, and `latest hits in the last 7 days` uses those 7 days.

Examples:

- `Show me the latest hits`
- `Latest hits for ABC1234`
- `Hits for ABC1234 last 30 days`
- `Any hits on plate 12ABC* yesterday`
- `Show recent hits for my org today`

You do not need a plate, driver, or kit — a time range, “recent”, or “latest” is enough. Otto searches hits scoped to your organization. Use hit search when you care about hotlist matches, not every plate read.
