# Scans on a case before assigning

Owners, managers, and office staff ask whether they already have scans before they assign a repossession order to an adjuster. Two common questions:

- Do I have any scans on this case number?
- Do I have any scans on this plate?

A **case number** is an **order**: the **external order ID** on a hotlist entry (the RDN case number). Scans are plate reads, so answer a case-number question by finding that hotlist entry, then searching scans for the plate on it. A plate question is a scan search for that plate.

A hit is a scan that already matched a hotlist entry. A scan question is about plate reads, including reads that did not become hits.
