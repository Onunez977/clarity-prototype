# Repossession adjusters

A **repossession adjuster** is the person in the truck recovering the vehicle. In the industry that work is also called **picking up metal**.

Adjusters are normally not logged into Clarity. **Owners, managers, and office staff** are the people who use Otto. They ask about orders and plates; the adjuster is the person they may send, not the person asking.
