# What is a scan?

A **scan** is a license plate read captured by a camera on a scan car. Each scan typically includes the plate, when it was seen, and where the vehicle was at that time.

The camera and the kit user are different. The camera name is the vehicle that captured the plate. The kit user is the person who was logged in. Cameras stay with the scan car; the person logged in can change.

Otto can search scans for your organization when you ask about a plate or a time window.
