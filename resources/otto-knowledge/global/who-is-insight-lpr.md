# Who is Insight LPR?

Insight LPR is the company that owns Platform and Clarity. It is the company, not a product and not a third party.

Platform and Clarity are software owned by Insight LPR. The public company site is https://insightlpr.com.

When someone says "Insight LPR," they mean this company.
