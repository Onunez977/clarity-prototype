# Kit user vs camera

A **kit user** is the person logged into the vehicle. **Kit ID** and **kit mapping** name that same person. If someone says **kid ID**, they mean **kit ID**.

A **scan car** is the vehicle. It typically carries four cameras — front and back, plus the left and right bumpers. **Camera name** is how that vehicle shows up on a scan. The cameras stay with the vehicle.

People change. Cameras do not. A different kit user can log in on the same scan car, and the camera names stay the same. One kit user can also log in on more than one vehicle over time.

A kit is not a camera. On a scan, the kit user is who was logged in, and the camera name is which vehicle captured the read.
