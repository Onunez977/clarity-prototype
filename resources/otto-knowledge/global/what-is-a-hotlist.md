# What is a hotlist?

A **hotlist** is an assignment — a vehicle your organization is looking for. It typically includes a plate, optional VIN and year/make/model, a status (open, hold, repossessed, closed), the client, and whether the assignment is **direct** (yours) or **staging** (forwarded to you). Customers call the entry’s **external order ID** an **order** or a **case number**. That value is the RDN case number.

When a kit captures a **scan** of a plate that matches a hotlist entry, Clarity creates a **hit**. Hits are the alerts operators review for recoveries and follow-up.

Ask Otto for hotlist or assignment search when you want the book of work. Ask for hit search when you want match activity.
