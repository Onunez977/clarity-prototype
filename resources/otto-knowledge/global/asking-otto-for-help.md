# Asking Otto for help

In this release, Otto can:

- Search **scans** and **hits** for your organization
- Search **hotlist assignments** (wanted vehicles, including VIN, status, client, and staging)
- Show interactive result tables in chat
- Look up **Scout driver locations** (when Scout is enabled for your org and you are an admin) in everyday place language
- Search **Scout patrol zones** (which zones you have, no-scan zones, and who a zone is assigned to) and propose creating or updating a zone
- Answer short product how-to questions from the Clarity knowledge base

You can ask for recent or today’s scans and hits with no plate or kit. Add a plate, VIN, assignment status, or driver/kit name when you want a narrower result. Ask which zones you have to list them in chat.
